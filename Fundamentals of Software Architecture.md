### ***Architectural Laws***
1. ***Everything in software architecture is a trade-off***
2. The ***Reason*** is more important than the ***Implementation***  (The WHY over the HOW).
3. Decisions in software architecture are not binary, usually involves more than 2 aspects. 

### ***What to expect from a Software Architect***
1. Taking architectural decisions
	- Choosing React to be the front-end framework is a technical decision.
