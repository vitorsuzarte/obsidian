The goal of these 4 weeks is:
- ✅ Diagnose every high school topic
- ✅ Identify structural weaknesses
- ✅ Raise reasoning level (not just procedural skill)
- ✅ Build teacher-level clarity (since you want to teach)

This is structured for someone who wants depth — not just “pass a test”.

---
# 🧠 STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

Each week has:
- **Diagnostic Phase**
- **Targeted Repair**
- **Advanced Layer**
- **Challenge Problems**
- **Reflection & Strength Mapping**

You’ll track everything in a **Strength Map Template** (provided below).

---
# 📅 WEEK 1 — Algebra Mastery

## Topics Covered
- Linear equations & inequalities
- Systems (substitution, elimination, interpretation)
- Polynomials & factoring
- Rational expressions
- Radicals
- Quadratics (all forms)
- Exponential & logarithmic equations
---
## Phase 1 (Day 1–2) — Baseline Diagnostic

Take:
- Khan Academy Algebra 1 Course Challenge
- Khan Academy Algebra 2 Course Challenge
Record:
- Score
- Time taken
- Types of mistakes
---
## Phase 2 (Day 3–5) — Weakness Repair

For each weak topic:
- Solve 20–30 focused exercises
- Then solve 5 mixed problems without notes
- Then explain the concept out loud as if teaching

If you struggle conceptually → use:
- **Art of Problem Solving** Alcumus Algebra
---
## Phase 3 (Day 6–7) — Advanced Layer

Solve:
- Hard systems problems
- Functional equations
- Parameter-based quadratics
- Exponential/log mixed problems

Then:  
Take 1 full **AMC 10 Algebra-heavy section** from  
Mathematical Association of America

---

AMC 10 or AMC 12 mixed exam.

# 🔥 WEEK 4 — Integration & Mathematical Maturity

This week is about **depth**.
## Day 1–3

Take 2 full AMC 10 exams under time pressure.
**Analyze deeply:**
- Why was I stuck?
- Concept gap or pattern recognition gap?
- Did I lack creativity or technique?
---
## Day 4–5 — Proof & Explanation

For weak areas:
- Write formal explanations
- Create your own problems
- Teach the concept (even to yourself)
---
## Day 6–7 — Final Assessment

Retake:
- 1 Khan challenge from your weakest domain
- 1 AMC exam
Compare results.
